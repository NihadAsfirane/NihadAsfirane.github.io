01 · MSc DissertationLJMU · 2026

Deep RL Control of a Grid-Tied Inverter

A deep Q-network (DQN) agent that directly controls the switching of a single-phase grid-tied H-bridge inverter, with no separate modulator and no system model. The Simulink model of the inverter and its LCL filter is driven from Python through the MATLAB Engine API. The trained controller reached a 5.45% current THD and a 2.69 A RMS tracking error on a 24.6 A peak reference, without a single overcurrent event.

Grid current (blue) tracking the reference current (dashed) under the trained DQN controller, over two grid cycles.
81/100 dissertation mark
